C0006 uniform positive thin-layer occupation — exact contract v2

Status: candidate. This is a standalone finite-system lemma, not a claim of completion of STATEMENT-v1. No promoted campaign result is a premise.

For every integer \(L\ge3\), let \(\mathbb T_L=\mathbb Z/(L\mathbb Z)\). For \(x,y\in\mathbb T_L\), define \[ \operatorname{dist}_L(x,y)=\min\{r,L-r\}, \] where \(r\in\{0,\ldots,L-1\}\) represents \(y-x\) modulo \(L\).

For every integer \(n\ge2\), the labelled state space is \(\mathbb T_L^n\). If \(z=(z_1,\ldots,z_n)\), then \(z+\varepsilon e_i\) means adding \(\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}\) to coordinate \(i\), modulo \(L\), leaving all other coordinates unchanged. On real functions \(F:\mathbb T_L^n\to\mathbb R\), define \[ R_nF(z)=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}} [F(z+\varepsilon e_i)-F(z)] \] and \[ I_nF(z)=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}} \sum_{\substack{j=1\\j\ne i}}^n \mathbf 1_{\{z_j=z_i+\varepsilon\}} [F(z+\varepsilon e_i)-F(z)]. \] For every \(q>0\) and \(d>0\), let \(Z=(Z_t)_{t\ge0}\) be the continuous-time Markov chain with generator \[ G_n=q(R_n+d^{-1}I_n). \] Thus each label has independent rate \(q\) to each of its two neighboring sites, plus rate \(q/d\) for each other label occupying the destination site. Both directions occur once; for \(L=3\) they are distinct. The parameters \(n\) and \(q\) are independent, with no scaling relation imposed.

For every integer \(k\) with \(1\le k<L/2\), define the positive-layer pair count \[ B_k(z)=\sum_{1\le i<j\le n} \mathbf 1_{\{0<\operatorname{dist}_L(z_i,z_j)\le k\}}. \] Pairs at equal sites are excluded.

For every deterministic initial state \(z\in\mathbb T_L^n\), and also for every initial distribution on that state space, equip the chain with its completed right-continuous natural filtration \((\mathcal F_t)_{t\ge0}\). For every deterministic real number \(\delta\ge0\) and every pair of bounded nonnegative stopping times \(\tau,\sigma\) satisfying \[ \tau\le\sigma\le\tau+\delta\quad\hbox{almost surely}, \] prove the conditional inequality \[ \mathbb E\left[\int_\tau^\sigma B_k(Z_s)\,ds \;\middle|\;\mathcal F_\tau\right] \le \binom n2\left[ \frac{2k\delta}{L+1/d}+\frac{Lk}{q} \right] \quad\hbox{almost surely}. \]

The bound has exactly the displayed constants and is uniform over all the stated parameters, initial states or distributions, and stopping times. No equilibrium, separated-start, or autonomy assumption for selected physical labels is permitted.

Required output: a complete proof of the displayed conditional inequality with every stated hypothesis, quantifier, constant, and object definition. There are no promoted dependencies, and no unproved candidate assertion may be used as a mathematical premise.
