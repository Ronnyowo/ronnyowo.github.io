Overview

This project records an AI-written proof of convergence from the inclusion process to a sticky Brownian mass flow. The setting is the one-dimensional torus. The initial state has a fixed finite number of stacks.

AI proof record. The source campaign labels the result “promoted” after its AI review procedure. No human review or outside-family model audit is recorded.

The small inclusion parameter is \(d_N\). The number of particles is \(N\). The cycle has \(L_N\) sites. The scaling is \[ \frac{N}{L_N}\longrightarrow\rho\in(0,\infty), \qquad Nd_N\longrightarrow\Theta\in(0,\infty). \] With time acceleration \(N^2/d_N\), the claimed limit concerns the full mass measure \[ \mu_N(t)=\frac1N\sum_x\eta_N(t,x)\delta_{x/L_N}. \] The target is the mass process associated with the Le Jan–Raimond sticky flow on the circle. The proof addresses Skorokhod \(J_1\) convergence, with a continuous limit. The exact statement gives all initial conditions and the full target definition.

Scope

The theorem permits vanishing normalized stack masses. Distinct initial stacks can have the same limiting position. The initial occupied sites must be separated by at least two lattice steps. The result concerns the torus. No full-line theorem is claimed.
